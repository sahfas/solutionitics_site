Our Story

Born From a Shared Vision

Solutionitics was founded in 2026 by Mohamed Sahfas and Sajaath Mohamed — two developers who share a belief that technology should empower businesses, not complicate them.

Having worked independently with agencies, startups, and global clients, we realized that many businesses struggle to find reliable, full-service development partners who truly understand both the technical and business sides.

So we joined forces. Combining full-stack web development expertise with deep Shopify and e-commerce knowledge, Solutionitics was built to be the kind of partner we always wished our clients had — transparent, technically excellent, and genuinely invested in results.
